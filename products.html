01 / THE CONNECTED CORE

MIPES engagement system

Multidomain Integrated Posture Engagement System brings simulated engagements, movement context, exercise control, and after-action review into one training environment. It is designed for focused drills and exercises involving thousands of simultaneous participants.

See more than the hit.

Laser engagement data is combined with posture and movement sensing. The resulting 3D reconstruction helps instructors examine positioning, transitions, and decisions during an exercise.

  • Dismounted vest and helmet sensing across five anatomical zones
  • Weapon and environmental effect simulation
  • Squad and armored vehicle participation
  • Exercise control, telemetry, statistics, and replay
  • Visual, audible, and smoke feedback options
System capabilities and brochure specifications +

Infantry equipment

Integrated vest and helmet sensor matrix; 180° frontal detection cone; response latency of 20 ms or less; 2800 mAh power modules; 12+ hours under standard mission loads.

Scenario elements

Weapon modules, dismounted participants, armored platforms, terrain and battlefield effects, and modular simulated munitions. Ballistics models account for trajectories and blast effects.

Exercise control

Asset allocation, scenario parameters, real-time telemetry, consolidated statistics, and full-motion tactical replay. Mobile base stations, GNSS, cellular connectivity, authenticated messaging, and anti-tamper features are described in the brochure.

Review

Multimodal event capture, posture and movement analysis, synchronized 3D after-action review, and training data integrity support evidence-led feedback.

02 / DYNAMIC OPPOSITION

ARTS robotic targets

The Autonomous Robotic Target System is a tracked target platform for live-fire and tactical simulation. A rotating multifunction target assembly, mobile chassis, positioning, scoring, and onboard behavior bring repeatable, responsive opposition into the exercise.

Movement that changes the scenario.

ARTS can navigate planned routes, respond to obstacles, vary target exposure, and react to adjudicated hits. Its integration with operator suits adds positional, posture, and logistics context to review data.

03 / POSTURE & HIT RESPONSE

Exposure and reaction that change with the action.

The target changes its forward lean to reflect movement speed. Its hit logic distinguishes fatal from nonfatal outcomes, then responds with a stop and drop or evasive movement and concealment.

High-speed posture
60° forward lean
Medium-speed posture

Low-speed / stationary
30° forward lean
Fatal-hit response
Immediate stop and mechanical drop
Nonfatal-hit response
Evasive rerouting and concealment
ARTS capabilities and brochure specifications +

Tracked mobility

Rugged chassis with 360° zero-radius pivot for outdoor and close-quarters terrain.

Autonomous navigation

Map-based route planning and dynamic obstacle avoidance reduce the need for continuous operator intervention.

Posture and hit response

Forward-leaning target postures of 60°, 45°, and 30° correspond to high-speed, medium-speed, and low-speed or stationary profiles. Fatal hits trigger stop and drop; nonfatal hits can trigger evasive rerouting and concealment.

Counter-engagement and telemetry

The brochure describes directional counter-engagement within a 20 m–3 km engagement envelope, plus suit-linked position, posture, communications, battery, and ammunition telemetry.

03 / PREPARATION & PRACTICE

Training accessories

Calibration, dry-fire, lighting, and responsive target products support preparation and focused practice alongside the connected training systems.

CALIBRATION / X-03

Multi-caliber laser boresighter

Tool-free alignment for optics and iron sights, with interchangeable adapters, red or green laser options, and common rifle, pistol, and shotgun caliber coverage.

Product details

Supports .22–.50 and 12GA/20GA. Stainless tail section; two CR-1/3N batteries. Brochure sighting ranges: indoor 5–100 yd, outdoor red 5–25 yd, outdoor green 5–50 yd. Quoted battery life: red up to 6 h, green up to 2 h.

CALIBRATION / X-04

Caliber-specific green boresighter

A daylight-visible, rechargeable option designed for field alignment and a 100-yard zero before live-fire confirmation.

Product details

Rifle and muzzle-device compatible; precision-machined stainless body; flashing green mode. Rechargeable 16340/CR123A 3.7 V battery; up to 8 h; outdoor sighting to 100 yd. Brochure lists .30, .22, 6.5 mm, .243, and .270 options.

DRY FIRE / DUAL MODE

Training + boresighting device

Combines dry-fire practice with in-barrel laser alignment in one stainless device.

Product details

Tool-free calibration and auto-eject function; rated for 10,000+ cycles. Three LR66 batteries; over 5,000 dry-fire shots or up to 1.5 h boresighting. Indoor 5–100 yd and outdoor 5–25 yd sighting ranges; multiple handgun, rifle, and shotgun caliber options.

DRY FIRE / LASER SNAP CAPS

Point-of-aim indicator

A trigger-activated red laser dot adds visual feedback while the snap cap protects the firing mechanism during function checks and demonstrations.

Product details

Stainless body, Class 3A laser module, smooth feed/cycle/eject function, three LR66 batteries, and over 5,000 shots. Offered in multiple calibers. The brochure specifies indication only; it is not intended for precision dry-fire training.

WEAPON LIGHT / GAL-B

Laser + LED illumination

A compact pistol-mounted module with laser, light, combined, and strobe modes for compatible accessory rails.

Product details

Fits Glock factory and Picatinny-compatible rails; mode memory; 500-lumen output; beam reach up to 100 m; up to 2.5 h combined runtime; two CR-1/3N batteries; IP68 water resistance.

RESPONSIVE TARGET / AUDIO

Audio-driven training system

Randomised sound cues and laser-activated targets create changing indoor or outdoor drills, including low-light practice.

Product details

Up to 30 sounds, visual and audible hit feedback, hit count and location, magnetic mounting, adaptive difficulty, and up to six targets. Rechargeable CR123A battery; brochure quotes up to 8 h of operation.
